Book - Liste des lieux

[Aile ouest] The western corridor is a room. The grammatical gender of the western corridor is masculine gender. The printed name of the western corridor is "couloir de l'aile ouest". The maid's bedroom is a room. The grammatical gender of the maid's bedroom is feminine gender. The printed name of the maid's bedroom is "chambre de la servante".
The laundress' bedroom is a room. The grammatical gender of the laundress' bedroom is feminine gender. The printed name of the laundress' bedroom is "ma chambre". The laundress' bedroom is proper-named.
The servants' quarters is a room. The grammatical gender of the servants' quarters is feminine gender. The printed name of the servants' quarters is "salle des domestiques".
